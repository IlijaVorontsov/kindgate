Why not just block it?

Blocking breeds wanting. Kindness breeds choice.

Kindgate is built on five findings from behavioural research. Each one shaped a screen.

Forbidden fruit

Tell children not to eat the red snacks and they eat more of them.

Restriction makes the restricted thing more attractive. Psychologists call it reactance. A hard block is a red-food rule.

Jansen, Mulkens & Jansen, 2007 · Kindgate never blocks; it asks.

The doughnut study

Guilt after a lapse causes the binge, not the lapse.

Restrictive eaters given one doughnut and a short self-compassion prompt ate less afterwards than those left to feel bad.

Adams & Leary, 2007 · The check-in logs a feeling with no verdict.

Urges pass

A craving peaks and fades within minutes, if you let it.

Riding an urge out beats fighting it. So instead of a flat no, Kindgate offers ten minutes.

Bowen & Marlatt, 2009 · The 10-minute delay button.

Decide earlier

Plans made in advance are kept far more often than plans made under temptation.

Implementation intentions have a medium-to-large effect on follow-through across hundreds of studies.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006 · Evening cards show your pre-set plans, one tap each.

Friction, not force

People want support that is not coercive, yet cannot be ignored.

A review of 367 digital self-control tools found that sweet spot is where tools actually help.

Lyngs et al., CHI 2019 · A short countdown on “continue anyway”.

Willpower runs out.
Kindness compounds.

What it does

YouTube, subscriptions only

Opens on Subscriptions. No Home feed, no Shorts, no recommendations, no autoplay. Search, channels and playlists keep working.

Instagram, people you follow

Locked to the Following feed. Reels, Explore, suggested posts and sponsored posts are gone.

A check-in, not a block

Every 15 posts a card shows how many posts and minutes went by. Keep going, or that’s enough. Your call.

Morning sequence

For the first half hour after waking, the phone shows your own morning steps instead of a feed. Nothing to decide.

Warm screen at night

After your set hour every page eases toward candlelight. Contrast survives, layouts never break.

Hands off to Shortcuts

Any button can run a shortcut of yours. Timers, reminders, whatever you already trust.

Private by construction

No account. No server. Nothing leaves the phone. The check-in log names only two sites, so it can never become a browsing history. You can copy it or clear it any time.
